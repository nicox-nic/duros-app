Create a premium Apple-inspired property management web and mobile app called “Landmasters PropertyOS.”

The app is an AI-powered property management system for condominium, subdivision, mixed-use, and commercial properties. It must directly communicate with a separate resident/homeowner/tenant app called Home AI, where residents can submit repair tickets, complaints, billing questions, work permits, gate passes, and special permit requests.

Design style:
Apple-like, premium, clean, professional, modern property management aesthetic. Use soft ivory backgrounds, deep charcoal typography, subtle champagne beige, soft blue-gray accents, glassmorphism cards, rounded corners, elegant spacing, soft shadows, and a low-opacity background image of a premium condominium lobby, property manager, engineering team, or modern residential building. The background image must be subtle and professional, around 5–12% opacity. The design should feel like Apple Business Manager meets high-end real estate operations.
